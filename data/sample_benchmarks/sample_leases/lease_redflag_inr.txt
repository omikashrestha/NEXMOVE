RESIDENTIAL RENTAL AGREEMENT (STRICT/RED-FLAG BENCHMARK)

This Rental Agreement is made and executed at Bengaluru on this 1st day of November 2026, by and between:
LANDLORD: Sri. M. Anand Murthy, residing at Indiranagar, Bengaluru.
TENANT: Relocating Professional ("Tenant").

1. PREMISES: The Landlord agrees to let out the 2BHK residential apartment situated at Indiranagar 100ft Road, Bengaluru.
2. TENURE: The tenure of this agreement shall be for 11 (eleven) months commencing from 1st November 2026.
3. RENT: The Tenant agrees to pay a monthly base rent of ₹38,000 (Rupees Thirty-Eight Thousand only), strictly payable by the 1st of every month. A late payment charge of ₹500 per day applies for delays beyond the 2nd.
4. SOCIETY MAINTENANCE: The Tenant shall pay monthly society maintenance of ₹4,500.
5. SECURITY DEPOSIT: The Tenant has paid a security deposit of ₹76,000 (Rupees Seventy-Six Thousand only).
6. LOCK-IN PERIOD: There shall be a strict lock-in period of 6 (six) months. If the Tenant vacates prior to 6 months, the entire security deposit shall be forfeited.
7. NOTICE PERIOD: 2 (two) months advance written notice required prior to vacating.
8. [CLAUSE 8 - MANDATORY REPAINTING DEDUCTION]: Irrespective of the duration of stay or condition of walls upon vacating, the Landlord shall deduct an unconditional, non-refundable sum equivalent to 1 (one) full month of rent (₹38,000) from the security deposit towards professional painting and deep sanitization.
9. [CLAUSE 12 - STRICT PET RESTRICTION]: No pets of any species or breed shall be harbored, sheltered, or allowed entry onto the premises under any circumstances. Any breach will result in immediate termination of the tenancy and forfeiture of the security deposit.
10. [CLAUSE 14 - ELEVATOR & MOVE-IN HOURS]: Freight movement, luggage shifting, and moving van loading/unloading are strictly restricted to the hours of 2:00 PM to 4:30 PM on official working days only. Violations incur a society penalty of ₹5,000.
