RESIDENTIAL RENTAL AGREEMENT (STANDARD BENCHMARK)

This Rental Agreement is made and executed at Bengaluru on this 1st day of November 2026, by and between:
LANDLORD: Sri. K. Ramesh Rao, residing at HSR Layout Sector 2, Bengaluru.
TENANT: Relocating Professional ("Tenant").

1. PREMISES: The Landlord agrees to let out the 2BHK residential flat situated at HSR Layout Sector 2, Bengaluru.
2. TENURE: The tenure of this agreement shall be for a period of 11 (eleven) months commencing from 1st November 2026.
3. RENT: The Tenant agrees to pay a monthly base rent of ₹30,000 (Rupees Thirty Thousand only), payable on or before the 5th of every English calendar month.
4. SOCIETY MAINTENANCE: The Tenant shall pay the monthly society maintenance charge of ₹3,500 directly to the Residents Welfare Association.
5. SECURITY DEPOSIT: The Tenant has deposited a refundable, interest-free security deposit of ₹60,000 (Rupees Sixty Thousand only, equivalent to two months' rent).
6. NOTICE PERIOD: Either party may terminate this agreement by providing 1 (one) month written notice.
7. LOCK-IN PERIOD: There shall be a minimum lock-in period of 3 (three) months from the commencement date.
8. PET POLICY: Domestic pets (dogs/cats) are permitted subject to society bylaws.
9. PAINTING & EXIT: On vacating the premises, the Tenant shall hand over the flat in broom-clean condition. Normal wear and tear is accepted; any physical damage beyond normal wear will be adjusted against the deposit.
10. JURISDICTION: Disputes are subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the civil courts at Bengaluru.
